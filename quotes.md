# Epigraph quote bank

A small, verified collection of opening quotations, grouped by theme. Pick one that
quietly echoes a post's idea and paste it at the very top of the body, just after the
front matter.

**Paste-ready format:**

```markdown
::: {.epigraph}
"Quote text here."

[— Author, *Work*]{.attribution}
:::
```

(Use `{.epigraph .epigraph-center}` for a centered version, as on the homepage.)

A note on honesty: where a line is traditionally attributed but has no firm primary
source, it is marked **(attributed)**. Prefer the sourced ones for anything you want to
stand behind.

---

## Intuition & discovery

> "It is by logic that we prove, but by intuition that we discover."
> — Henri Poincaré, *Science and Method* (1908). *In use: welcome post.*
> Good for: anything about building intuition before formalism.

> "We must know. We shall know." *(Wir müssen wissen. Wir werden wissen.)*
> — David Hilbert, address to the Society of German Scientists and Physicians, Königsberg (1930).
> Good for: open problems, foundations, a hopeful note on the unknown.

## Unity & abstraction

> "Mathematics is the art of giving the same name to different things."
> — Henri Poincaré. *In use: spectral theorem post.*
> Good for: classification, isomorphism, "these all turn out to be the same object."

> "The introduction of numbers as coordinates ... is an act of violence."
> — Hermann Weyl, *Philosophy of Mathematics and Natural Science* (1949). *In use: homepage.*
> Good for: coordinates vs. coordinate-free thinking, geometry over computation.

## Freedom & creation

> "The essence of mathematics lies in its freedom."
> — Georg Cantor, *Grundlagen einer allgemeinen Mannigfaltigkeitslehre* (1883).
> Good for: set theory, new constructions, foundations, anything that invents its own objects.

## Beauty & art

> "A mathematician, like a painter or a poet, is a maker of patterns. If his patterns are more permanent than theirs, it is because they are made with ideas."
> — G. H. Hardy, *A Mathematician's Apology* (1940).
> Good for: structure, elegance, pure mathematics, pattern-finding.

> "Mathematics, rightly viewed, possesses not only truth, but supreme beauty — a beauty cold and austere, like that of sculpture."
> — Bertrand Russell, *The Study of Mathematics* (1902; repr. in *Mysticism and Logic*).
> Good for: a post where the result is simply beautiful.

## Symmetry & structure

> "Symmetry, as wide or as narrow as you may define its meaning, is one idea by which man through the ages has tried to comprehend and create order, beauty, and perfection."
> — Hermann Weyl, *Symmetry* (1952).
> Good for: group theory, invariants, symmetry, conservation laws.

## Nature & application

> "Philosophy is written in this grand book, the universe ... It is written in the language of mathematics, and its characters are triangles, circles, and other geometric figures."
> — Galileo Galilei, *Il Saggiatore (The Assayer)* (1623).
> Good for: applied mathematics, modelling, mathematical physics.

## Problem-solving & inversion

> "Invert, always invert." *(Man muss immer umkehren.)*
> — Carl Gustav Jacob Jacobi **(attributed)**.
> Good for: duality, working backward, contrapositive, reformulating a hard problem.

## Rigor, with a wink

> "In mathematics you don't understand things. You just get used to them."
> — John von Neumann **(attributed)**.
> Good for: a hard technical post, or a humble note on a counterintuitive result.
