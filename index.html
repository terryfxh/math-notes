Math Notes

A place where I try to see mathematics, not just compute it.

“The introduction of numbers as coordinates … is an act of violence.”

Hermann Weyl — Philosophy of Mathematics and Natural Science

Most of the time, mathematics gets handed to us as a list of things to calculate. I have always been far more curious about the picture sitting underneath all that machinery: the reason a theorem has no choice but to be true, the quiet moment when a row of abstract symbols suddenly turns into something you can picture and almost touch.

This is where I work that out in the open. Each post starts from the simplest version of an idea I can find, builds up the intuition until the result feels inevitable, and only then writes down the formal proof. Where a picture or a few lines of runnable code make the idea easier to believe, I include them.

I write these mostly for the version of me who was once stuck on the very same thing. If you happen to be stuck there too, I hope they help.
